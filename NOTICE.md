- The logic in `src/ap.inc` is generated from the region files of the [SMS apworld](https://github.com/Joshark/archipelago-sms) by Joshark, a fork of the Archipelago repository, whose LICENSE is the MIT License (copyright LLCoolDave, Berserker66, CaitSith2, LegendaryLinux).
- The tracker's markers (`src/tracker.inc`) come from the [Sunshine_Pop](https://github.com/LiterallyNoOne1/Sunshine_Pop) PopTracker pack (MIT; LiterallyNoOne1, UmedMuzl, Sparky5000, Schwarts Gandhi, JXJacob). Its maps (by VGCartographer) are in `mod/tracker/images/maps`, with the pack's license in `mod/tracker/LICENSE-Sunshine_Pop` (MIT, copyright UmedMuzl 2025). The maps are renders of Nintendo's game; the MIT license is the pack's.
- The idea of the randomizer (the unlocks, the entrance shuffle) is TheAzack9's Kuribo module; no code of it is used.
